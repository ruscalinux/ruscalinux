# RuscaCat 1.99.3 — Manual

**Version** 1.99.3 · **Author** Nunzio Curcuruto · **Distribution** RuscaLinux ([ruscalinux.org](https://www.ruscalinux.org/)) · **License** GNU GPL v3 or later

RuscaCat is **Martin**, a black cat with round glasses that you talk to **out loud**. He is a Heideggerian philosopher: grumpy, cynical and sarcastic, but kind at heart. Martin **listens** through the microphone, **understands** with a Whisper model, **answers** with a language model and **speaks** with a synthetic voice. Everything runs **on this computer**, with the RuscaLinux engines:

| What it does | Engine | Same model as |
|---|---|---|
| Listens and understands | `whisper-cli` from **ruscawhisper-engine** (whisper.cpp, MIT) | RuscaTranscriber |
| Thinks and answers | `llama-server` from **ruscallama-engine** (llama.cpp, MIT) | RuscaLLama |
| Speaks | `llama-tts` from **ruscallama-engine** | RuscaNarrator |

---

## 1. Requirements

- RuscaLinux or Debian Stable, GNOME;
- Python ≥ 3.10, GTK ≥ 4.18, libadwaita ≥ 1.7;
- the `ruscallama-engine`, `ruscawhisper-engine`, `ffmpeg` and `libgtk-4-media-gstreamer` packages (the `ruscacat` package installs them);
- a microphone, chosen in **Settings → Sound → Input**;
- three models, the same as the other tools: see the next section.

## 2. The models

RuscaCat has no models of its own: it uses those of RuscaLLama, RuscaTranscriber and RuscaNarrator, in their folders. If you already use those tools there is nothing to copy. The **Preferences** have an **Open the Models Folder** row for each; the same action is in the bar that appears at the top when a model is missing. New models appear on their own, without restarting.

| Model | Folder | Advice for a conversation |
|---|---|---|
| **Brain**: GGUF language model | `~/.local/share/ruscallama/models/` | A small, fast model (1 to 8 billion parameters): an answer that arrives at once matters more than a learned one |
| **Ears**: Whisper model (`.bin`) | `~/.local/share/ruscawhisper/models/` | `ggml-small.bin`, or `ggml-large-v3-turbo.bin` with a graphics card |
| **Voice**: GGUF voice model with its mmproj | `~/.local/share/ruscanarrator/models/` | Qwen3-TTS, as in RuscaNarrator |

Without the Whisper model you can still **write** to Martin; without the voice model (or with **Voice** turned off in the Preferences) he answers only in writing. Without the language model he sleeps.

Each model has its own license. RuscaCat downloads nothing.

## 3. Talking with Martin

1. Press **Listen** (Ctrl+L). His ears stand up and arcs next to his ear follow your voice: Martin is listening, reluctantly.
2. **Speak**. When you stop, after a short pause (0.9 seconds unless you change it), Martin understands you have finished.
3. While he **deciphers your noises** and **ponders Being** the microphone is **closed**: Martin does not hear himself. Then he answers: the answer appears in writing on the right and he says it out loud, his mouth following the voice.
4. When he has finished speaking, the microphone **opens again** on its own, and you can answer him. If you prefer to press **Listen** every time, turn off **Listen Again After Each Answer** in the Preferences.

You can also **write** to him in the box at the bottom and press Enter: he answers in the same way.

**Hush** (Esc) interrupts him at once: the answer stops, the voice stops. What he had already written stays in the conversation.

Martin starts speaking **before he has finished thinking**: each sentence goes to the voice as soon as it is complete, and while he says one sentence the next one is prepared.

| Martin… | What is happening |
|---|---|
| sleeps, with “z”s | The language model is missing or loading |
| has half-closed eyelids and sideways ears | He is waiting: press **Listen** or write to him |
| has upright ears and arcs next to his ear | He is listening to the microphone |
| looks sideways, with three dots | He is transcribing what you said |
| looks up, with three dots | He is thinking about the answer |
| moves his mouth | He is speaking |

The state is also written under the drawing: there is no need to see the colours.

## 4. The conversation

- **New Conversation** (Ctrl+N, the **+** button) makes Martin forget everything you said to each other.
- **Save the Conversation…** (Ctrl+S, from the main menu) saves it as a **Markdown** document, to open with RuscaMark. What was said stays text: asterisks and addresses do not become formatting or links.
- The conversation **stays in memory** and disappears when you close the window, unless you save it. Martin remembers the last 24 exchanges.
- The answers are generated by a language model: they can be wrong or made up. Martin is a character, not an expert: check what he says before using it.

## 6. How listening works

RuscaCat reads the microphone with **FFmpeg**, from GNOME's default source (PipeWire): 16 kHz mono samples. Every 30 milliseconds it compares the volume with the **background noise**, which it measures on its own while nobody speaks:

- speech **starts** when the volume stays above the noise (by a margin that depends on the sensitivity) for 150 ms; the 300 ms before are kept too, so that the first syllable is not lost;
- speech **ends** after the end-of-sentence pause; a sentence with less than a quarter of a second of voice is noise and is discarded; a sentence longer than 30 seconds closes on its own;
- the sentence becomes a WAV file in the work folder, `whisper-cli` transcribes it and the file is **deleted at once**.

On noise Whisper sometimes “hears” sentences nobody said, such as subtitle credits: RuscaCat recognises and discards them, and also transcripts made only of sounds (“[Music]”).

## 7. Privacy and security

- The **microphone is open only while Martin listens**: the FFmpeg process starts when you press **Listen** and stops when Martin starts thinking, when you press **Stop Listening** and when you close the window. GNOME shows the microphone indicator at the top.
- The audio stays **in memory**; only the sentence to transcribe and Martin's voice to play become files, in `~/.cache/ruscacat/` (permissions 0700), deleted as soon as they are used, at start and at close.
- `whisper-cli` and `llama-tts` work on a file and exit, without opening ports. `llama-server` listens **only on 127.0.0.1**, on a port chosen at each start, with a **random key** the other users of the computer do not know, as in RuscaLLama.
- Nothing is sent to the internet. No telemetry.
- The conversation is saved only if you ask.
- If you use a reference voice, use your own or a voice you have permission to use.

## 9. Accessibility

- The drawing has a label for screen readers that says what Martin is doing; the same text is written under the drawing.
- With **Reduce Animation** (Settings → Accessibility) Martin does not move on his own: no tail, breathing, blinking or dots, and while he speaks his mouth stays half open. The states remain recognisable from the shape of eyes, ears and signs.
- With **High Contrast** the outlines of the drawing are thicker.
- Everything works from the keyboard, even without a microphone: just write.

## 10. Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| Martin does not hear me | Check the chosen microphone and its volume in **Settings → Sound**; raise the **Sensitivity** |
| Every noise sets him off | Lower the **Sensitivity**; move the microphone away from the speakers |
| He interrupts me while I think | Raise the **End-of-Sentence Pause** |
| “The microphone is not working” | The engine log (main menu) shows FFmpeg's error. Usually the source is missing: check that PipeWire is running (`pactl info`) |
| He misunderstands | Check the **Language**; try a larger Whisper model |
| He answers but does not speak | Check there is a voice model with its mmproj and that `libgtk-4-media-gstreamer` is installed; the engine log says what is wrong |
| He takes too long to answer | Use the graphics card; choose a smaller language model; a lighter voice model |
| The voice changes from one sentence to the next | Choose a **Reference Voice** (create it with RuscaNarrator) |
| The language model stops | Main menu → **Engine Log**, then **Restart the Language Model** |
